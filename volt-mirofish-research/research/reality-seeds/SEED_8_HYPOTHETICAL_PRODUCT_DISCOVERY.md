### 1. The Discovery Event
A popular tech discussion forum and developer community (featuring software engineers, startup founders, freelance collective leaders, operations managers, and tech skeptics) discovers a viral post:

> **Title:** *"Show HN / Reddit: Volt – We replaced hourly timesheets with a Git-like CLI and mathematical impact scoring ($C^{1.4} \times I \times E$). Here is why presence is a broken metric."*
> 
> **Post Body:**
> *"We got tired of timesheets. Fast thinkers and slow thinkers sit at desks for the same 8 hours, but one solves an architectural bottleneck in 20 minutes while the other churns boilerplate. Hourly pay punishes skill and rewards presence.*
> 
> *We built **Volt** (`volt.qmatus.com`):*
> *1. `volt add` stages micro-tasks in `<50ms` from your terminal or AI IDE.*
> *2. `volt commit` bundles staged work with cognitive complexity, business impact, and verifiable GitHub PR proofs.*
> *3. A deterministic mathematical engine and optional AI judge compute points against your manager’s written rubric.*
> *4. The team bonus pool distributes directly according to verified points.*
> 
> *Live at `volt.qmatus.com`. CLI and MCP available. Thoughts? Is output-based compensation feasible, or is it destined to be gamed?"*

### 2. Immediate Community Dynamics & Discussion Vectors
- **Vector 1: The Frustrated High Performer:** Celebrates the death of timesheets; loves terminal and MCP integration.
- **Vector 2: The Skeptical Systems Engineer:** Immediately questions gaming vectors, prompt manipulation of commit summaries, and the validity of $C^{1.4}$.
- **Vector 3: The Startup Founder:** Evaluates whether this reduces management overhead and aligns teams with business goals or creates toxic internal competition.
- **Vector 4: The Privacy & Labor Advocate:** Warns about automated algorithmic evaluation of human labor and potential surveillance creep.
- **Vector 5: The Pragmatic Engineering Manager:** Inquires about configuration burden, custom rubrics, and how to handle non-code contributions.
