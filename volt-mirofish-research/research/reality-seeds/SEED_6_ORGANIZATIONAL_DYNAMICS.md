# REALITY SEED 6: ORGANIZATIONAL DYNAMICS
**Domain:** Cross-Role Perspectives, Hierarchy, Power Shifts & Team Alignment  
**Integrity Level:** Verified Sociological Patterns in High-Tech Firms  

---

### 1. The Founder & C-Suite Perspective
- **Core Motivation:** Maximizing company velocity, eliminating deadweight, and aligning team incentives directly with high-leverage business milestones.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Does Volt give me true visibility into who is actually moving the needle versus who is just performing busyness? Does it protect our runway while rewarding top performers?
- **Concerns:** Culture damage if employees feel surveilled or if peer competition harms collaboration; administrative overhead if rubrics require continuous tweaking.

### 2. The Engineering Manager / Team Lead Perspective
- **Core Motivation:** Maintaining team morale, delivering sprint commitments, and conducting fair, defensible performance reviews without spending weekends compiling status reports.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Does Volt provide objective proof to support compensation adjustments and promotions? Can I easily override AI mistakes without tedious bureaucracy?
- **Concerns:** Having to arbitrate disputes between engineers arguing over complexity ratings ($C=5$ vs. $C=8$) or co-author splits.

### 3. The Senior Engineer & Tech Lead Perspective
- **Core Motivation:** Deep technical excellence, autonomy, eliminating interruptions, and fair compensation for hard architectural and research breakthroughs.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Does the CLI get out of my way? Does it recognize that spending 3 days disproving an architectural dead end is just as valuable as writing 500 lines of boilerplate?
- **Concerns:** Will the AI misinterpret deep code complexity? Will shallow developers who write elaborate commit summaries game the model better than quiet, high-leverage engineers?

### 4. The Junior / Mid-Level Contributor Perspective
- **Core Motivation:** Skill growth, job security, clear mentorship, and predictable compensation.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Can I earn a fair bonus if most of my assigned work is essential bug fixing and maintenance ($C=1, 2$)?
- **Concerns:** Feeling marginalized by a steep non-linear scoring power curve ($C^{1.4}$) that concentrates rewards among senior architects.

### 5. Finance & Operations Decision-Makers
- **Core Motivation:** Fiscal predictability, ROI on software tooling, and clean payroll reconciliation.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Is the bonus pool calculation deterministic and auditable? Does it integrate cleanly with Stripe/Gusto/Deel or will it create an accounting nightmare?
