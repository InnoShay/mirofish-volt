# REALITY SEED 5: ADOPTION AND RESISTANCE
**Domain:** Drivers of Adoption, Points of Friction & Rejection Mechanics  
**Integrity Level:** Verified Dynamics in B2B SaaS & Developer Tooling  

---

### 1. Primary Adoption Catalysts (Why Teams Choose Volt)
- **Deep Friction Reduction:** Developers who resent opening web timesheets embrace the terminal CLI (`volt add`) and AI IDE integration (`volt-mcp`), requiring $<3$ seconds to stage work.
- **Fairness for High Performers:** 10x engineers, senior architects, and fast problem solvers see an avenue to get compensated for breakthrough output rather than logging idle hours.
- **Objective Manager Visibility:** Founders and managers gain a real-time, tamper-proof audit trail of verified deliverables (GitHub PRs, benchmarks) without having to hover over employees.
- **Alignment with Incentive Pools:** Clear mathematical distribution of bonus pools gives teams skin in the game and direct financial upside when complex goals are shipped.

### 2. Primary Friction Points & Resistance Drivers (Why Teams Reject Volt)
- **Goodhart's Law & Gaming Concerns:** When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure. Skeptics argue engineers will game Volt by framing trivial fixes as high-complexity ($C=8$), inflating impact ratings, or aggressively co-authoring to duplicate points.
- **The "AI Performance Appraisal" Taboo:** Many employees and managers have a visceral negative reaction to an AI LLM deciding work value or bonus shares. The phrase "AI Judge" triggers intense skepticism regarding bias, hallucinations, and dehumanization.
- **Non-Technical Team Exclusion:** While developers love Git CLI metaphors (`add`, `commit`), non-technical members (sales, HR, customer success) may find the workflow alienating and confusing.
- **Zero-Sum Bonus Anxiety:** If the bonus pool is fixed, one teammate's high-scoring commit reduces everyone else's percentage share. This can corrode camaraderie, leading to hoarding of high-complexity tasks and cutthroat internal competition.
- **Legal & HR Compliance Hurdles:** Salaried employment contracts in many jurisdictions require standard overtime, minimum wage, or fixed pay structures. Variable mathematical weightage requires careful legal separation into discretionary bonus pools.
