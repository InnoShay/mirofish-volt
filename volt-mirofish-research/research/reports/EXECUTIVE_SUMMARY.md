## 1. STRATEGIC CONTEXT & RESEARCH MANDATE
Volt was engineered as an ambitious, output-based contribution tracking and mathematical weightage platform designed to replace legacy desk-hour timesheets with a Git-inspired staging and commit paradigm:
$$\text{Micro-tasks} \xrightarrow{\texttt{volt add}} \text{Staging Buffer} \xrightarrow{\texttt{volt commit}} \text{Proof \& AI Weightage } (W_c = C^{1.4} \times I \times E \times V \times D)$$

This research project leveraged **MiroFish** as a clean-room, simulated-reality research laboratory. Zero code, database, or network integrations were made into Volt's production environment. Instead, MiroFish executed three controlled multi-agent experiments to probe how technical communities, founders, engineering managers, contractors, and labor skeptics perceive, stress-test, adopt, or reject Volt.

---

## 2. HIGH-LEVEL EMPIRICAL FINDINGS ACROSS EXPERIMENTS

### Finding 1: Universal Praise for Client Ergonomics & Speed vs. Deep Skepticism of Algorithmic Payroll
- **Observation:** Across all three experiments, technical agents unanimously applauded the `<5ms` local SQLite write-ahead queue, the terminal CLI (`volt add`), and the absence of Electron bloat. 
- **The Core Paradox:** The moment the system connected those fast logs to **direct financial compensation** ($C^{1.4} \times I \times E$) or an **AI Judge**, sentiment shifted sharply from enthusiastic curiosity to defensive alarm.
- **Evidence:** Agent 0 (Alex Rivera, Systems Eng) in Exp A; Agent 25 (Simon Kestler, Tech Creator) in Exp C; Agent 24 (Victor Strand, Industry Analyst) in Exp B.

### Finding 2: The "Goodhart's Law" Vector & "Prompt Engineering of Commits"
- **Observation:** The single strongest intellectual critique raised across both technical and management personas is that codifying complexity into an exponential curve ($C^{1.4}$) inevitably incentivizes superficial verbosity.
- **The Concern:** Quiet, high-leverage engineers squashing deep concurrency bugs or reducing technical debt will be penalized, while articulate verbalizers who describe routine tasks in grandiose architectural terms will capture disproportionate points.
- **Evidence:** Agent 4 (Martin Lindqvist, SRE) in Exp B & C; Agent 27 (Bret Holloway, Teardown Author) in Exp C.

### Finding 3: The "Digital Taylorism" and Surveillance Fear
- **Observation:** In low-trust or traditional corporate environments, logging every micro-task into an immutable PostgreSQL ledger is perceived not as developer empowerment, but as modern, automated surveillance.
- **Evidence:** Agent 19 (Gordon Vance, Privacy Advocate) and Agent 23 (Claire Dupont, Agile Coach) in Exp B.

### Finding 4: Cross-Functional Alienation (The Non-Engineer Barrier)
- **Observation:** Product designers (Agent 13, Chloe Martin), growth marketers (Agent 16, Rachel Adams), and junior developers (Agent 15, Lucas Morales) expressed acute alienation toward Git-specific metaphors (`staging`, `commit`, GitHub PR verification).
- **Evidence:** Agent 13 in Exp A & C; Agent 15 in Exp A & C.

### Finding 5: Validation of the Recent Strategic Pivot (Docs 14-16)
- **Observation:** When presented with the latest product direction—decoupling from automated payouts, introducing dual time-based and VoltAI scoring, and empowering managers with plain-language rubrics—decision-makers (founders, VPs, ops) shifted from categorical rejection to cautious interest.
- **Evidence:** Agent 9 (Wei Chen, VP Eng) and Agent 6 (Marcus Vance, Founder) in Exp C.

## 4. STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS FOR VOLT EXECUTIVE TEAM
1. **Rebrand from "AI Payroll Judge" to "Founder & Manager Delivery Cockpit":** Double down on the Doc 14 direction. Never market Volt as an algorithm that decides salaries or bonuses. Position it as an objective fact-aggregation tool that helps managers see verified deliverables and unblock team goals.
2. **Elevate Refactoring & "Negative Space" Engineering:** Introduce explicit rubric starter templates that weight technical debt reduction, refactoring, and hypothesis refutation equally to feature PRs.
3. **Bridge Non-Technical Roles:** Broaden client interfaces beyond the CLI. Emphasize the Tauri desktop overlay, Telegram audio notes, and direct Figma/Google Docs artifact attachments.
4. **Preserve Team Psychological Safety:** Default bonus shares to hidden/advisory only. Ensure individual points are never displayed on public team leaderboards.
