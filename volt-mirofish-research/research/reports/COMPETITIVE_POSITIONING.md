## 2. DIRECT COMPARISONS AGAINST INCUMBENT CATEGORIES

### 1. Volt vs. Traditional Timesheets (Harvest, Toggl, Clockify)
- **The Competitor Workflow:** Start timer $\rightarrow$ forget to stop timer $\rightarrow$ manually adjust timesheet Friday at 5 PM $\rightarrow$ submit fabricated 40 hours.
- **Volt's Advantage:** Real-time micro-capture in $<50$ms (`volt add`) from CLI/MCP; non-linear complexity awareness ($C^{1.4}$) so an expert 15-minute fix is rewarded properly.
- **Agent Verdict:** Overwhelming preference for Volt's capture speed (Agents 0, 1, 25).
- **Vulnerability:** Clients who demand standard hourly invoices cannot easily parse "complexity points" without translation (Agent 2, Agent 18).

### 2. Volt vs. Surveillance Tools (Hubstaff, Time Doctor)
- **The Competitor Workflow:** Background daemon monitoring keystrokes, taking periodic desktop screenshots, logging active window titles.
- **Volt's Advantage:** Absolute rejection of physical presence monitoring; respects developer agency; relies solely on output deliverables and cryptographic PR verification.
- **Agent Verdict:** Unanimous agreement that Volt is infinitely more ethical than Hubstaff, but vigilance is required so managers do not abuse the immutable ledger as "digital Taylorism" (Agent 19, 23).

### 3. Volt vs. Engineering Metrics Platforms (LinearB, Jellyfish, DX)
- **The Competitor Workflow:** Ingests GitHub/Jira to produce aggregate cycle time, DORA metrics, and PR velocity charts for executive reviews ($20–$50/seat/mo).
- **Volt's Advantage:** Connects directly to individual developer capture tools (CLI, MCP, Tauri) and translates verified proof into team bonus pool allocations rather than abstract aggregate graphs.
- **Agent Verdict:** Technical leads (Agent 9, Wei Chen) recognized Volt as more actionable at the squad level, but cautioned that LinearB avoids Goodhart's Law by deliberately separating metrics from compensation.

### 4. Volt vs. Modern Issue Trackers (Linear, Jira)
- **The Competitor Workflow:** Agile sprint planning, Kanban boards, story point estimates (Fibonacci) established *before* work starts.
- **Volt's Advantage:** Focuses on what was *empirically delivered* (complexity $C$, impact $I$, evidence $E$) rather than speculative pre-sprint planning estimates.
- **Agent Verdict:** Agile advocates (Agent 23, Claire Dupont) argued that story points must remain team planning estimates, praising Volt only if it operates as a separate post-delivery retrospective layer.
